How do I earn Bhakti tokens?

Only by practice. Finishing a session you set a goal for gives 3 tokens plus 2 for every full mala (108 jaap); a session stopped early gives 1 token per full mala. Tokens can't be bought and never expire. They are kept online with your account and count once your session has uploaded.

How do I unlock Ram, Narad and Shiv?

They open by themselves after 6, 12 and 24 hours of jaap practice, or you can unlock them sooner with Bhakti tokens (110, 220 and 430). In the app, an unlocked Aaradhya is downloaded once (up to about 11 MB) when you tap Download, and then works offline.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. Jaap, mala, timers and streaks all work offline. Sessions upload automatically when you're back online. Unlocking and downloading an Aaradhya needs a connection.

I reinstalled the app and my progress is gone

Your progress belongs to a random account on the phone you used; we never ask for an email or phone number. A fresh install starts a new account, so there is no way to sign back in to the old one. Please keep the app installed when changing settings or updating.

Who sees me on the leaderboard?

Nobody, unless you turn on Show me on the list in the Streaks tab. Even then you appear only as a numbered name such as “Bhakt #4821” with your jaap total — never your name or photo.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings → Delete my data and confirm. Your account, sessions, tokens, unlocks and leaderboard entry are deleted from our server straight away and everything on your phone is cleared. See the privacy policy for details.
