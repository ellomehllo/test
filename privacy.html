Privacy policy

Aradhya (the app and vanarsathi.online) · Last updated 24 October 2026

In short: you never give us your name, email or phone number. The app records how much jaap you do, under a random account number, so your counts, streaks and Bhakti tokens are kept safe. No ads, no tracking, nothing sold. You can delete everything at any time from Settings → Delete my data.

What we keep on our server

  • A random account ID. Created automatically the first time you open the app. It isn't linked to your name, email, phone number or device ID.
  • Your practice sessions. For each jaap session: the number of jaap, the minutes, whether you completed your goal, your phone's local date and the time it was saved.
  • Your Aaradhya unlocks. Which Aaradhya you have unlocked and whether by practice or with Bhakti tokens. Your token balance is calculated from your sessions.
  • Your leaderboard choice. Whether you want to appear on the leaderboard (off unless you turn it on).

What stays only on your phone

  • The name and photo you set on your profile. They are never uploaded.
  • Your settings, chosen Aaradhya and mantra, and your detailed session history.
  • Downloaded Aaradhya animations.

The leaderboard

If you turn it on, other people see a numbered name such as “Bhakt #4821” and your jaap total for the chosen period. They never see your profile name, photo or anything else about you. Turn it off at any time in the Streaks tab.

Voice counting (optional)

If you switch on voice counting, the app asks for microphone permission and counts your naam using your phone's built-in speech recognition. We don't receive or store any audio. Depending on your phone, its speech recognition service (for example Apple or Google) may process the audio under that company's own privacy policy.

Who helps us run Aradhya

  • Supabase stores the account ID, sessions and unlocks described above (servers in Tokyo, Japan) and serves the Aaradhya animations you download.
  • Cloudflare hosts vanarsathi.online and, like any web host, briefly processes your IP address to deliver the pages.
  • On the website only, fonts come from Google Fonts and one script from jsDelivr, which receive your IP address when they load. The app has these files built in.

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your data until you delete it. In the app, open Settings → Delete my data and confirm: your account, sessions, unlocks and leaderboard entry are deleted from our database straight away, and everything the app stored on your phone is cleared. This can't be undone. If you uninstall the app without deleting, your account can no longer be reached from any phone; contact us (below) if you'd like it removed.

Children

Aradhya is a devotional practice app suitable for all ages. It doesn't ask for any personal information and has no chat or messaging.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we'll update this page and the date at the top. Questions or requests: support@vanarsathi.online · Help & contact
