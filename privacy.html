Privacy policy

Sadhana (the app and vanarsathi.online) · Last updated 25 October 2026

In short: you never have to give us your email or phone number. The app records how much jaap you do, under a random account number, so your counts, streaks and Bhakti tokens are kept safe. The name you choose for your profile is shown on the public leaderboard. No ads, no tracking, nothing sold. You can delete everything at any time from Settings → Delete my data.

What we keep on our server

  • A random account ID. Created automatically the first time you open the app. It isn't linked to your name, email, phone number or device ID.
  • Your practice sessions. For each jaap session: the number of jaap, the minutes, whether you completed your goal, your phone's local date and the time it was saved.
  • Your Aaradhya unlocks. Which Aaradhya you have unlocked and whether by practice or with Bhakti tokens. Your token balance is calculated from your sessions, purchases and unlocks.
  • Your profile name. The name you write on your profile, shown to everyone on the leaderboard. Leave it empty to appear as a numbered Bhakt.
  • Your leaderboard choice. Whether you appear on the leaderboard (on unless you turn it off).
  • Name reports. If you report someone's name, we keep that you reported it until the report is dealt with.
  • Token purchases. If you buy Bhakti tokens in the app: the App Store transaction number, the pack and the date, so the tokens reach your account exactly once. Payment itself is handled by Apple; we never see your card or Apple Account details.

What stays only on your phone

  • The photo you set on your profile. It is never uploaded.
  • Your settings, chosen Aaradhya and mantra, and your detailed session history.
  • Downloaded Aaradhya animations.
  • On iPhone, the numbers the home-screen widget and the lock-screen session view show (today's jaap, goal, streak, the running count).

Bhakti tokens and purchases

Bhakti tokens are earned by practising and can also be bought in the iPhone app as one-time packs. They are only for unlocking Aaradhya inside Sadhana: they have no cash value, can't be exchanged or moved to another account, and don't expire while your account exists.

Purchases are made through your Apple Account and are handled entirely by Apple. We only receive the transaction number, the pack and the date, which we use to add the tokens to your account exactly once. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com; if Apple refunds a purchase, the tokens it added may be removed.

The leaderboard

Everyone on the leaderboard sees your profile name (or a numbered name such as “Bhakt #4821” if you haven't chosen one) and your jaap total for the chosen period. They never see your photo or anything else about you. Hide yourself from it at any time in the Streaks tab.

Names that are abusive, contain links or contact details, or pretend to be the app are refused. Any name can be reported from the leaderboard; a reported name is hidden for the person who reported it, and a name reported by several people is removed. To report a name directly, write to support@vanarsathi.online.

Voice counting (optional)

If you switch on voice counting, the app asks for microphone permission and counts your naam using speech recognition built into your phone. We never receive, record or store any audio.

  • iPhone app: Apple's speech recognition, on the phone itself wherever the phone supports it for the language; otherwise Apple processes the audio under its own privacy policy.
  • Android app: recognition runs entirely on the phone, offline.
  • Website: your browser's speech recognition, which may send audio to its maker (Google for Chrome, Apple for Safari).

Who helps us run Sadhana

  • Supabase stores the account ID, sessions and unlocks described above (servers in Tokyo, Japan) and serves the Aaradhya animations you download.
  • Apple handles in-app payments and, on iPhone, speech recognition as described above.
  • Cloudflare hosts vanarsathi.online and, like any web host, briefly processes your IP address to deliver the pages.
  • On the website only, fonts come from Google Fonts and one script from jsDelivr, which receive your IP address when they load. The app has these files built in.

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your data until you delete it. In the app, open Settings → Delete my data and confirm: your account, sessions, unlocks, token balance (including bought tokens) and leaderboard entry are deleted from our database straight away, and everything the app stored on your phone is cleared. This can't be undone. If you uninstall the app without deleting, your account can no longer be reached from any phone; contact us (below) if you'd like it removed.

Children

Sadhana is a devotional practice app suitable for all ages. It doesn't ask for any personal information and has no chat or messaging.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we'll update this page and the date at the top. Questions or requests: support@vanarsathi.online · Help & contact
